Artificial Intelligence (AI) is transforming the way people live, work, and learn. It helps computers perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as understanding language, recognizing images, and making decisions. AI is used in many fields, including education, healthcare, business, and transportation. Students can use AI-powered tools to learn new concepts, while businesses use AI to improve efficiency and customer service. As technology continues to advance, AI is expected to play an even greater role in solving complex problems and creating new opportunities for people around the world.
